# Reproducible combat research

Choose scenarios that answer the user's question, rather than automatically staging a mirrored stationary duel. Specify fits, skill assumptions, positions/velocities, ammunition reserve, horizon, seed and controller policy. The default stationary-weapons-v1 policy locks and fires conventional guns/missiles; it does not supply movement, repairs, EWAR or drone behavior. Other behavior requires an explicit supported policy and successful native admission. Unsupported mechanisms are gaps, not permission to simulate an invented approximation.

Use stable jobId for retry. run/prepare require each ship's sessionId/revision. Waiting can last up to 60 seconds per call; follow next rather than resubmitting start. failed is execution failure, partial is a checkpoint, and only complete is a completed horizon. The CLI keeps the host alive for jobs it starts; do not launch a legacy short-lived --call start then exit.

Reports include the native request/scenario identity and, for new task-API runs, bound fitting snapshots and declared conditions. expectedDraftId detects accidental report substitution. Historical unbound reports cannot be combined with a new fitting's DPS/EHP without independently verifying correspondence. If a new experiment fails, say so before discussing historical evidence.

HP snapshots apply only at their attached times. Last-damage HP, opponent-destruction time and end-of-simulation HP are distinct; exact simultaneous HP can be unavailable. A first missile-application sample is not an average and its multiplier is not hit probability. A shorter kill time is not a universal “ship is X% stronger” finding. Report time-to-kill reduction separately from inverse-time efficiency if calculating either from the returned evidence.

Use jobs storage/list for capacity and job discovery. New runs retain bounded recovery generations. If cleanup is within the user's task, preview jobs compact at its revision, inspect what will be reclaimed, then apply dryRun:false to the selected inactive job. This only removes redundant recovery payload; it preserves final results or the resumable point, and keeps job identities. Do not delete native files through a shell, enlarge quotas silently, or clean unrelated jobs merely because next suggests cleanup. An old-version job requires its original engine for numerical replay; listing/manifest/compaction do not certify replay compatibility.

Inspect `battle policies` (optionally draftId) before changing a fitting to satisfy a controller. `stationary-weapons-defense-v1` drives conventional weapons and engine-admitted conventional hardeners, with native capacitor/cycle costs. It does not control propulsion, local sensors, repairs, EWAR or fighters. Keep the requested fit; select an appropriate explicit policy or report a limitation instead of silently replacing active modules with passive ones.

Use citableFacts for destruction and last-damage statements. Exact unavailable simultaneous HP must not be inferred from a nearby snapshot. Neither subtracting total HP from capacity nor dividing it by time establishes measured sustained DPS, because resistance, passive recharge and overkill intervene. unavailableClaims explicitly identifies report conclusions that have not been calculated.
